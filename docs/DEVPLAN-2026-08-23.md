# LLMDeals Development Plan — August 23, 2026

## The product in one sentence

> **LLMDeals tells you the best AI deals available right now, how good they actually are, who they are good for, and shows the evidence/math behind the verdict.**

---

## Homepage design

Hero: "The best AI deals right now"

Top 5 cards (not 3000 models):
- #1 Best overall
- #2 Best completely free
- #3 Best API deal
- #4 Best premium-quality deal
- #5 Best specialist deal

Today's Deal Spotlight section.

Full searchable database on /deals page.

Deal detail pages with verdict, best for, catch, math, evidence.

## Consumer taxonomy

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Free | genuinely useful recurring free access |
| Promo | temporary discount/credit |
| Subscription | pay monthly for discounted usage |
| Credit | spend X/get Y |
| Unlock | payment/balance unlocks benefits |

## Editorial ratings

Exceptional / Excellent / Good / Niche / Weak

Components: Economics, Model quality, Usability, Confidence

## Database schema

providers, products, models, offers, deal_routes, evidence, assessments, spotlights
